# Donations

Everything here is free and open source. If a plugin is useful to you and you'd like to help
support the time that goes into building, porting, testing and maintaining them, contributions
are very welcome, and never required.

- Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/sd88me (one-off or monthly, no account needed)
- GitHub Sponsors: https://github.com/sponsors/sd88me (coming soon)

Donations support development time. They don't buy access to, or unlock, any firmware, ROMs or
third-party content, and nothing is gated behind them.

Thank you!
